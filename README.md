# Dribble Lab

A camera-based dribble trainer test app (proof of concept). Open https://icetap4455.github.io/dribble-lab in Chrome.

- Tracks a brightly colored ball by its color and the player's body with MediaPipe Pose, all on the device.
- Counts dribbles and shows their height, the hand used, and whether the bounce landed in the target box.
- Developer mode (tap the logo 5 times): desk object, touch, robot player, replay of recorded clips, and a debug screen.

No video or results leave the device.
