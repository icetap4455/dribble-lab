How to test
  1. Put the phone sideways on its stand about 3 feet up and 8 to 10 feet away. His whole body, feet included, should be on screen.
  2. Press Start camera and allow the camera. Then press Tap ball to set color and tap the middle of the ball. Do this again whenever the lighting changes.
  3. Check for a yellow circle on the ball, blue lines on his body, and an orange box beside his right foot.
  4. Pick a test, press Start test, and press Record clip. Dribble for 1 minute, then stop recording.
  5. Count by hand: open developer mode (tap the Dribble Lab logo 5 times), choose Replay, load the clip at 0.5x, and press +1 for each bounce. Then press Save result.
  6. When all 6 tests are done, press Copy results and paste them in our chat.

Pass: at least 95% match on every test, and the fps badge stays green (24 or more).
